Everyone who enters the same room code joins the same race. Share the code with your friends.

How to Play
  1. Every race has 6 new horses with Speed, Stamina and Rider XP. Weather, track and injuries change how much each one matters.
  2. Type an amount or tap a chip, then tap Bet next to a horse. You can also type a bet like 75 on 3.
  3. If your horse wins you get your bet times its odds. The odds come from its win chance.
  4. Lucky snacks add luck to your bet, but supplies are limited.
  5. With friends, everyone sees the same horses and race, and anyone can start it. Solo, computer players bet too.
  6. Spend your bank in the Avatar Shop on coats, manes, hats and more for your profile picture.
  7. Races take 30 seconds around the track at normal speed. Change the race speed (0.5x to 3x) above the track. In a room, only the host can change it, and the race begins once every player has pressed Start race. Tap Cheer to make some noise for your horse. Cheering is just for fun and never changes the result.
  8. Add friends by swapping friend cards. They work without any sign-in.
  9. Casual starts you with $1000 that never saves, and if you go broke the bank stakes you $500. Competitive uses your saved bank with no bailout. Claim hourly ($100 to $200) and daily ($1000 to $2000) rewards, and finish daily and weekly quests, in the Rewards & Quests menu.
